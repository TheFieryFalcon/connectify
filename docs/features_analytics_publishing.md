# Connectify Analytics, Predictor & Testing Features Reference

This document details user-facing features 11 through 20, covering Goals and Estimates, ATAR progression graphs, Weakness Radar, Settings & Calibration, DOM sweeping, Grade notifications, store publishing, Predictions (Predict Grade / Predict ATAR), canonical sidebar sequence, and the master test suite.

## 7. Front-Facing Features Directory (Part 2: Features 11–20)

### 11. Goals & Estimates
- **Source Modules**: [`target-atar-ui.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/src/atar/target-atar-ui.js), [`target-grade-ui.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/src/atar/target-grade-ui.js), [`grade-estimate-ui.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/src/atar/grade-estimate-ui.js), [`atar-ui.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/src/atar/atar-ui.js)
- **DOM Insertion**: One calculator panel (`#connectify-atar`) in the sidebar workspace, opened by two launchers: **Goal Setter** (Target Grade, Target ATAR) and **Estimator** (Estimate Grade, Estimate ATAR).
- **UI Appearance**: Launcher name as heading, then the Predictor-style mode tabs (`.cta-mode-tabs`), the semester buttons and the active mode's view; tabs name their mode (`Target Grade` / `Target ATAR`, `Estimate Grade` / `Estimate ATAR`), Grade first; Target Grade's and Target ATAR's semester buttons read plain `Semester N`; each launcher opens on Grade, then reopens on its last tab.
- **Functionality**:
  - **Estimate ATAR**: Aggregates scaled scores across enrolled ATAR courses using TISC Average Marks Scaling (each course's 2025 scaled mean + SD x z, z from the standardised mean 60 / SD 14); selects top 4 subjects for Tertiary Entrance Aggregate (TEA); applies 10% bonus for Methods, Specialist, and Languages (up to 2 bonus courses); calculates projected ATAR. Each course takes a school mark (Connect's by default) and shows its scaled score; a semester with final grades is Closed.
  - **Target ATAR Planner**: Input target ATAR; toggles top 4 course inclusion; difficulty-weighted optimization adjusts required marks based on historical strengths across subjects and categories (`Adjust marks by subject & task type performance`).
  - **Target Grade Planner**: Select course, enter target percentage, dynamic prior performance adjustment option (`Dynamically adjust required grade based on prior performance`), clean semester tabs eliminating ATAR text.
  - **Goals**: **Set as Goal** under Target Grade / Target ATAR opens on the planner itself (End beside the target: Semester 1 / 2 or a custom school week, which narrows the planner's table; Confirm in the button's place), its sliders still live; `Goals (n)` lists saved goals (Edit reopens the planner, Delete, Past goals). A plan for a subject with a goal reads **Update Goal** and changes that goal, keeping its judged tasks and history; only Delete starts over. Effort sliders (5 notches) shift required marks between tasks or subjects; tiers Trivial–Hell from the chance of reaching the target, Guaranteed / Impossible lock in Achieved / Missed. Outline rows get a goal-performance bar and a goal-progress bar; a newly read result (outline expanded) brings a Goal Updated toast naming the tier; a goal that ends brings Goal Achieved (green accent, with a celebration) or Goal Missed (gray accent). See `modules_goals.md`.
  - **Estimate Grade**: Select a class; each weighted unmarked task gets a mark input (`85`, `85%`, `17/20`, `17 Out of 20`). One headline (current average, else the estimate and its change) updates as you type, with no notes: Connect's average for the marked tasks as one block plus each typed mark at its weight (`ConnectifyGradeEstimate.estimate`); blank tasks are left out, so one typed mark is enough, and typing every one gives the final mark. Unweighted tasks get no input; unreadable marks are flagged (`aria-invalid`) and ignored. Typed marks persist per class (`gradeEstimates`) until Clear Marks.

### 12. ATAR Progression & Historical Graphs
- **Source Modules**: [`progress-chart.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/progress-chart.js), [`progress-graph.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/progress-graph.js)
- **DOM Insertion**: Full-screen modal overlay (`#connectify-progress`).
- **UI Appearance**: Responsive SVG line chart plotting student marks (blue polyline) vs cohort mean (red polyline) over school weeks (subject scale 50–100%, or from the lowest result or cohort mean below 50% rounded down to the nearest 10), split by a dashed `Semester 1 | Semester 2` line on every graph (tasks, months or assessment rounds) with detailed task data table listing completed and pending tasks (grayed out with $\ge 5:1$ contrast).
- **Progress Graph tab**: "Show:" dropdown plots single tasks, a running weighted average or a weighted rolling average of the last three tasks (cohort mean alike); cohort means survive the semester filter (derived from cached boxplots); interactive hover tooltips; a table of each task's weight, mark and cohort mean, no dates.
- **Next Up tab** (between Progress Graph and Task Dates): this week's and next week's assessments, each with its subject, type, weight, Low / Mid / High prediction (its grade once marked) and the mark a goal asks of it with its difficulty; a week with nothing in it shows a happy face: All Done! in the holidays or once no task is dated later, else No Tasks This Week / Next Week.
- **Task Dates tab**: term starts with the Early Easter checkbox on one line (a week past Term 1's end then reads as Term 2's), and every task with its week, subject, type (the same editable dropdown as the outline rows; a change in either place shows in both) and grade (week: `Term 2 Wk 5`; ✎ to override, opening on that week, `No week` and an empty input without one). `Group By` (None, Subject, Type, Completed, Predicted Grade, Grade Achieved; High Distinction (A) from 80%, Distinction (B) from 70%, Credit (C) from 60%, Pass (C) from 50%, Fail (D)), an arrow for its direction, `Sort By` (Chronological, Alphabetical, Grade Achieved/Predicted Grade) and `Show Uncompleted Only`. Grouped by Predicted Grade, the grade column shows each task's prediction. A second row's Subjects and Type dropdowns tick each one on or off, also for Export's downloads.
- **Export**: a toggle on that same list (uncompleted tasks only), Assessment and Date staying put while Day and Export Date replace the right half: a tick and weekday per task, the subject's weekday when grouped by Subject, the export date, optional merge of each semester's exams into one event in both files; `Google Calendar (.ics)` and `Notion (CSV)` download them, made locally (no sign-in, no new permissions). Spec: `specs/progress-calendar-export.md`.

### 13. Weaknesses Radar Chart
- **Measure**: by type, how each result sits against its own subject's average (so a weak subject never reads as a weak type); the cohort's means as a dashed line beside the student's. Tasks with no type are left out and counted.
- **Source Module**: [`weakness-radar.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/weakness-radar.js)
- **DOM Insertion**: Standalone panel (`#connectify-weakness`).
- **UI Appearance**: Native SVG polygonal radar chart with 5 concentric percentage rings (20% to 100%), radial spoke axes, green performance polygon (`#2ecc71`), and perimeter labels.
- **Functionality**: Plots performance by assessment category; interactive subject checkboxes (`#cx-weakness-checkboxes`) and type checkboxes under them (`#cx-weakness-type-checkboxes`), each with Select/Deselect All; persists filters in `connectify:weakness_disabled_subjects` and `connectify:weakness_disabled_types`.

### 14. Settings & Scaling Calibration Panel
- **Source Modules**: [`category-settings.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/category-settings.js), [`scaling-calibration.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/scaling-calibration.js)
- **DOM Insertion**: Standalone panel (`#connectify-categories`).
- **UI Appearance**: Three distinct sections: General Preferences (`connectify:auto_expand`, `connectify:general_cohort_size`, `connectify:atar_percentage`), Semester 1 Scaling Calibration grid table (`connectify:preferences`), and Assessment Categories editor (`+ Add Category`, custom colors, keyword editor).
- **Functionality**: Synchronizes custom categories across storage; triggers `rescanAllAutoAssessments()` upon saving.
- **Statistics Panels** (`connectify:panel_view`): Compact view (default), Expanded view on subject panels only, or Expanded view on all panels; takes effect after reload.
- **Ignore Certificate and General subjects** (`connectify:hide_course_stats`, on by default): outlines with Certificate, Cert or General in their name stay as Connect drew them, with no statistics or prediction panels, Type dropdowns, meters or goal bars, and the class is no option in any tool (Goal Setter, Predictor, Year Progression, Estimator, Weakness Analyzer, the export, Jump To, Settings' subject lists). Its results feed no prediction and it raises no notification. A goal on one rests until the option is off. Applies at once.
- **Hide potentially inaccurate prediction bars** (`connectify:hide_early_meters`, off by default): outcome meters wait for three earlier results in the subject (or a subject baseline) instead of one. Applies at once.
- **Backup**: Export Data saves everything Connectify keeps for the student (goals, types, weeks, calibration, baselines, categories, past years and this year's marks, themes, preferences) to `connectify_backup_<date>.json`; Import Data lists what the file holds and what importing does here, with anything that would replace data unticked, then reloads (`modules_past_years.md`).
- **Show this semester's classes only** (`connectify:semester_filter`, off by default; always the detected semester: Semester 2 from school week 17 by the term calendar; a new school year, even one begun in Term 4, is Semester 1 until its own): hides the other semester's outlines, refreshes the cache from this semester's cards only (the other semester's cached results stay frozen and are still used, e.g. final Semester 1 marks) and lists only this semester's classes in every sidebar tool. Filtering the outlines to a class keeps its outline for this semester visible; a class with none shows a message saying its other-semester outline is hidden, with "← Back to All".

### 16. Grade Update Notifications & Jump-to-Subject
- **Source Modules**: [`notifications.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/notifications.js), [`new-grade.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/new-grade.js)
- **DOM Insertion**: Fixed bottom-right toast stack (`#connectify-notifications-container`).
- **UI Appearance**: Purple-accented toast card (`.cx-notification--grade`) with trending icon (`📈`), updated average, delta percentage, and "Jump to Subject" action button.
- **Functionality**: Tracks running subject averages in `connectify:grade_cache:<student>`; detects grade updates once summary marks are shown (own running mark moves $\ge 0.05\%$ against the same semester's cached mark; classmates' results moving the boxplot never notify; cache-version bumps keep the cache), or a task newly marked on an outline whose rows are shown (catches a result that leaves the mark unchanged; with Auto-expand on, every visit); marks the subject stale for re-scraping on its next expansion; "Jump to Subject" expands it, scrolls to a newly marked task (any one; the subject average row when none is known) and pulses an outline glow around the whole subject card, and on the task too (`.cx-card-jump-highlight`).

### 17. Multi-Store Publishing & Automated CI/CD (AMO & Chrome Web Store)
- **Source Modules**: [`.github/workflows/publish.yml`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/.github/workflows/publish.yml), [`.github/scripts/publish-amo.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/.github/scripts/publish-amo.js), [`CHROMEWEBSTORE.md`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/CHROMEWEBSTORE.md), [`PRIVACY.md`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/PRIVACY.md)
- **Functionality**: GitHub releases (a `v<version>-<commit time>` prerelease per main push, plus `latest`, both titled and noted as development builds not to install; whose older builds are deleted after each upload: GitHub caps a release at 1000 assets) are checked, created and uploaded through `gh` calls that retry GitHub server errors (HTTP 5xx, timeouts) four times and fail with GitHub's own message; automated AMO v5 REST API upload via HS256 JWT authentication; Chrome Web Store clean packaging without prohibited keys or dev artifacts, uploaded through CWS API v2 (`mnao305/chrome-extension-upload@v7`, needs secrets `CWS_CLIENT_ID`/`CWS_CLIENT_SECRET`/`CWS_REFRESH_TOKEN` and `CWS_PUBLISHER_ID`; the CWS step condition reads the job-level `env`); standardized multi-dimension icons (16, 48, 128); minimal `storage` permission footprint (`unlimitedStorage`, which lets GitHub/Firefox builds keep GIF wallpapers up to 40 MB, is stripped from the CWS zip by both workflows, as are the update-reload background script and its Connect `host_permissions`, which refresh open Connect tabs after an update in GitHub/Firefox builds).

### 18. Predictions (Predict Grade, Predict ATAR) & Dynamic Breakout Performance Meter
- **Source Modules**: [`predictor-math.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/predictor-math.js), [`predictor-ui.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/predictor-ui.js), [`cohort-view.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/cohort-view.js), [`category-settings.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/category-settings.js)
- **DOM Insertion**: Launcher button `Predictor` (`#connectify-predictor-toggle`), workspace panel (`#connectify-predictor`), vertical outcome meter (`.connectify-outcome-bar`), and "Previous Year Baselines" card in Settings.
- **Functionality**:
  - **Cold-Start Baselines**: Previous-year category and subject averages kickstart predictions; dynamically injects baseline inputs for custom categories.
  - **Logarithmic Ceiling**: Tapers gains above 80% proportionally to headroom $\frac{100 - \text{Score}}{20}$.
  - **Predict Grade**: one scrollable sub-tab per subject (no cap, so a dropped course plus its replacement still shows every class) with Low, Mid, High task estimates. Secret purple breakout threshold is strictly hidden. Unparsable dates excluded unless custom override exists in Progress Graph.
  - **Predict ATAR**: Aggregates projected subject marks into Low, Mid, High ATAR and TEA without Year 11 scaling deductions, with a disclaimer at the bottom (school marks only; also on Estimate ATAR).
  - **Dynamic Breakout Performance Meter**: 18px wide, 56px tall; 4 baseline segments: Red ($< \text{Low}$), Orange ($\text{Low} \le S < \text{Mid}$), Yellow ($\text{Mid} \le S < \text{High}$), Green ($S \ge \text{High}$). Suppressed on incomplete tasks and on a subject's first result without a baseline; Settings "Hide potentially inaccurate prediction bars" (off by default) holds back the second and third results too. Secret double-height Purple breakout segment (height 20px) appears when $S > 1.10 \times \text{High}$. Critical shortfall ($S \le \text{Low} - 10\%$ additive) renders 0 segments with pulsing red alarm glow (`.connectify-outcome-critical`).

### 19. Sidebar Integration & Theme Navigation Stabilization
- **Canonical Sequence**: Goal Setter, Predictor, Year Progression, Estimator, Weakness Analyzer, Past Years (once a year is archived), Settings.
- **Button Styling**: Settings (`#connectify-categories-toggle`) and Weakness Analyzer (`#connectify-weakness-toggle`) feature white buttons (`#ffffff`) in light mode with centered text.
- **Category Colors**: Category labels bind to `--cx-cat-color` via `.cx-cat-name-label` for vibrant visibility in dark mode.
- **Theme Button Mount**: `#connectify-theme-toggle` auto-discovers `.cvr-c-primary-navigation` and centers at `top: 50%; transform: translateY(-50%)`.

### 20. Master Unified Test Suite (`test_suite.js`)
- **Source Module**: [`test_suite.js`](file:///Users/uwong/Downloads/2.1.14_0/test_suite.js)
- **Role**: Single definitive master test suite across the extension. All new tests must be added to this file only. Run via `node test_suite.js`.
- **Test Sections**:
  - Section 1: Dark Mode Site-Wide Coverage & Contrast Hierarchy ($\ge 5:1$).
  - Section 2: Outcome Meter & Hover Tooltip Reliability (pointer-events, sizing, tiers).
  - Section 3: Chronological Historical Prediction Engine & Caching (headroom, variance, versioning).
  - Section 4: Sidebar Navigation & Workspace Layout (order, expansion, categories).
  - Section 5: Grade Update Notifications & System Integrity (delta formatting, jump-to-card, lifecycle).
  - Section 6: Regression Verification (centering, first-assessment outcome rules, card scraping, predictor interactivity).
  - Playground (`npm run playground`, `scripts/playground/`, Test 279): a local page at the outlines path (`localhost:4173`) running every outlines-group script and Connectify's own component stylesheets (no theme or Connect styles; toolbar toggle) from the manifest after a `chrome.storage` / `runtime` shim. Its editor (not Connect markup) creates classes (generated subject, two tasks per semester, weights 100) and edits type, name, term/week, weight, mark, cohort mean, order, final grades and overall marks; `model.js` writes them as the scraper would cache them (subjects cache with parser-format ids, fixed through edits; class registry, Semester 2 listing every task) and fires `connectify-results-updated`, so goals, toasts and every sidebar tool react. Release next result marks the earliest unmarked task; scenarios export/import as JSON. Outline-row features (panels, meters, goal bars) need Connect's rows and are not shown; week changes reach goals after Reload (week snapshot).
  - Script login (`scripts/puppeteer-auth.js` / `puppeteer-utils.js`): a saved session runs headless (launch waits for the extension's install reload, which aborted the first navigation); otherwise a login window opens behind the app in front (macOS gets its focus back), with Connect's Student sign-in already chosen (`chooseStudentLogin`), then the run continues headless.
  - Theme audit (`scripts/test-theme-styles.js`): applies themes through `connectify-request-theme` (the registry is isolated-world), clears focus before each capture, composites contrast over the theme's own `--cx-canvas-bg`, counts unblurred one-axis inset shadows (accent bars) as separators, and counts a side margin only if the box moved (Connect's centred empty state reports 0 natively, 230px themed, in the same place; Test 321). The sidebar is opened and audited on Assessment Outlines only (`isOutlinesUrl`, Test 319); other pages get page views. It visits every Year Progression subtab read from the page (Progress Graph per subject, Next Up, Task Dates: plain, grouped, the Subjects and Type lists open, Export) and Set as Goal on the planners; `view.steps` (`click`, `select`, `press` to a state) wait for what they act on, are idempotent and re-run on each theme pass. Views with the same elements are audited once, across every URL (`viewKey`, Test 320); closed semesters get no view.
  - Liquid Glass transparency and blur (`scripts/audit-glass.js`, Test 318): on both glass presets `measureGlass` (page world) reads each layer's fill, stacked fills and blur (colour functions such as `oklch()` by painting a pixel, as the contrast checks do), `judgeGlass` (Node) reports `GLASS_TOO_TRANSPARENT`, `GLASS_TOO_OPAQUE`, `GLASS_WITHOUT_BLUR`, `GLASS_BLUR_TOO_WEAK`, `GLASS_TOO_BLURRY` and `NESTED_BACKDROP_BLUR` against the theme's own `--cx-surface-*` alphas (+-10%; inside a surface one 12% tint) and `--cx-glass-blur` (`--cx-glass-blur-overlay` on layers over text), drawer included; solid dialogs must be opaque, backdrops dim 20-85%. The theme menu and Jump To list are opened and judged once per page. Listed under "Liquid Glass Transparency & Blur" in the report.
  - Glass surface structure (Test 180): `NESTED_SURFACE_FILL` (a fill > 12% inside another surface) and `ROUNDED_FULL_WIDTH_BAR`, both glass presets; controls, chips, avatars, active states, overlays and Connectify UI exempt.
  - Contrast coverage and tool data (Test 185): SVG `<text>`/`<tspan>` (chart labels) are measured by fill against the CSS background behind the `<svg>` plus SVG shapes painted under them; Connectify UI on page views (cohort panels, type selects, outcome meters, theme menu) skips parity but is contrast-checked; `TOOL_SUBJECTS_MISSING` (once per drawer tool) flags Predictor tabs, Weakness checkboxes or Progress chips missing a subject with marked tasks, listed under "Tool Data Completeness" in the report.
  - Keyed ancestors (Test 219): no `src/` stylesheet tests a class substring (`[class*=...]`) on an ancestor or inside `:not(... *)`, so Connect's class changes restyle only what they can affect.
  - Spacing parity (Test 160): class/id containers with 0px padding/margin in `connect_css/` (the Default theme) must not get non-zero spacing from any theme or custom theme stylesheet.
  - Keyed theme rules (Test 270): every theme stylesheet is already expanded by `scripts/expand-is.js` (running it changes nothing), and the expansion keeps matches and specificity.
