# Connectify Design Rules & Style Standards

Extremely concise summary of the core visual design language, contrast standards, component geometry, and interaction patterns across Connectify.

---

## 1. Color Palette & Hierarchy
- **Canvas / Background**: Light `#f7f9fc`, Dark `#12171f`, Quantum Dark `#282828` (Gruvbox bright green `#b8bb26` highlights), AMOLED `#000000`, Midnight `#0b132b`, Forest `#0a1914`, Liquid Glass (grey `#aeb4c2` canvas, `#8d99a6` panels at 30%) and Liquid Tinted Glass (navy `#0b1120`) diffusion.
- **Top Navigation & Menus**: Light `#ffffff` / `#e9f2fb`, Dark `#1a222d`.
- **Course Tiles (`.eds-c-tile`)**: Light `#ffffff` (border `#d8e3ee`), Dark `#1e2632` (border `#2e3c4e`, hover `#3d5066`). Translucent frosted glass (`rgba(18, 26, 40, 0.65)`) in Liquid Glass / custom background.
- **Cohort Statistics Panel (`.connectea-panel`)**: Deep navy `#15263a`, border `#264669`, text `#d6e8f8`.
- **Summary Marks Box (`.cvr-c-task__marks`)**: Box `#161e29`, divider `#2d3b4d`.
- **Accordions & Section Headers**: `#222b39`, border `#2e3c4e`; transparent over custom wallpapers or Liquid Glass.
- **Category Colors**: Exam (Red `#e74c3c`), Test (Green `#2ecc71`), Application (Blue `#3498db`), Essay (Purple `#9b59b6`), Take-Home (Yellow `#f1c40f`); defined once in `src/cohort/category-store.js`. Labels bind to `--cx-cat-color` via `.cx-cat-name-label`.
- **Important Tiles** (`.eds-c-tile--important`, e.g. Maintenance Notice; `theme-alert-tiles.css`): keep Connect's red in every dark theme: `__header--negative` tinted (`#dc2626` 16%, or Connect's `#9d000b` 10% in light tone), red header divider, red `.eds-t-red` icons (`#f87171`, `#9d000b` in light tone, incl. `-webkit-text-fill-color`).
- **Assessment Type Label**: Always formatted as `"Type: "` with trailing colon and space.

## 2. Contrast & Typography Standards
- **WCAG AA Threshold**: Minimum **5:1 contrast ratio** across all foreground text and backgrounds in both light and dark modes.
- **Liquid Glass & Panel Geometry**: 16px corner curvature (20px on modal dialogs) applies **only in Liquid Glass** (`[data-connectea-theme="liquid-glass"]` or `[data-connectea-glass="true"]`), on explicit panel, card, tile and modal classes, never `[class*=...]` substring matches. Clipping is limited to tiles and cards (`overflow: clip`, never `hidden`: hidden stops a last child's margin collapsing out of the tile, which drew an extra strip under the Maintenance Notice), never popups or tooltips. All other themes keep Connect's native geometry. Tile bodies are transparent in Liquid Glass. Non-Liquid-Glass panels are strictly opaque (`var(--cx-surface-bg, #1e2632) !important;`) even with custom wallpapers active. Liquid Glass **must NOT** alter the styling, border-radius, or geometry of buttons, inputs or expansion-panel headers; positive (View) buttons stay square like native Connect. Angular panels theme.css cannot name by class are rounded in `theme-glass-panels.css`: the `cnt-stream-notice-card` host (no clip, so the elevation shadow survives) and the Library table wrapper. **No contrast panels**: bars, banners and headings Connect leaves flat or surface-less (`cnt-scope-intro-class`, category bars, Library toolbar, Select All row, `p.mat-h2` headings, empty states, paginators; Light mode tabs, headers and toolbars over a wallpaper) get no panel, pill or card of their own (`theme-glass-surface.css`, `theme-control-bars.css`): they sit straight on the page, and the wallpaper is dimmed or brightened for the theme's text instead (Wallpaper Layer). Blocks inside a glass card stay clear with hairline dividers (`theme-glass-class-pages.css`). **One surface per block**, in every dark theme (`theme-surface-nesting.css`): only the outermost painted element fills; everything inside it (lists, tables and their rows/cells, activity feeds, menu lists and rows, accordion panels, fieldsets, tab headers in cards, stat cells, cohort panel lines, the article wrapper around a home page tile, a promo inside a tile with no border or corners of its own) is clear, so no text sits on a box of a different shade; hover and active states keep their highlight. In Liquid Glass (`theme-glass-nesting.css`) only the outermost surface also blurs, because stacked translucent fills turn opaque and a blurred parent stops a child's blur reaching the page. The header app's canvas is clear so the top bars blur the page. Assessment task rows (`theme-glass-task-rows.css`) sit inside the glass tile, so the statistics strip, the overall stats panel and the achievement column (marks and boxplot) are a 7% text-colour tint with a hairline, no fill or blur of their own (in every dark theme the chart background and Connect's white decoration shapes stay invisible, only the box keeps a fill); their text uses the primary ink. Rules that must beat id-weighted or `!important` Connect selectors use the unused `#cx-specificity-anchor` inside `:is()`. Paginators and the Select All / Download row are clear (no fill, edge, shadow or pill) in every dark theme and in Light with a wallpaper; their labels keep the theme ink (`#334155` in Light). Former panel elements keep native margins, padding, borders and sizes, and clipped table wrappers use `overflow: clip` (hidden changes margin collapsing). Empty states are centred in every dark theme by alignment only (`theme-empty-states.css`, no spacing changes).
- **Theme Dropdown & Customizer**: Sleek 28px height with 12x12 chevron and expanded 4px 6px padding (`.connectea-theme-arrow`). In-DOM Theme Customizer (`#cx-customizer-modal`) is styled by the active theme's tokens like the rest of the site (its headings and labels take the theme ink in every theme, Light included, never Connect's heading or label colours) on an always-solid dialog (surface layered over the opaque canvas, never translucent in glass themes) (only its live preview uses the draft palette) with Basic (6) & Full (20) modes; every token derives from the 6 Basic ones in two layers (Basic -> Full -> every other `--cx-*`, `theme-token-derive.js`) using the presets' own relationships: OKLCH lightness steps that keep the base hue and chroma/lightness ratio, flipping towards the text on light themes. Disabled and placeholder text follow the presets (no 5:1 floor); derived primary, secondary, muted, link and primary-button text keep >= 5:1, but a text token the user sets is used exactly as set (never contrast-swapped); ink on fills with no text token (accent, primary button, avatar) is the theme's own text when it reaches 5:1. **Full-mode tokens the user sets win over the Basic tokens** in both modes (switching to Basic never re-derives them; ↺ resets one); themes save Basic tokens + those overrides. Picking a preset starts with no overrides except the Full tokens the preset keeps (`keeps`: Quantum Dark's green secondary text). The colour picker's drag never selects text or scrolls the page (pointer capture, default cancelled). Live preview renders every Full token, interactive HSV/HEX color picker in a popover beside the token being edited (below it, or above when there is no room; closes on outside click, its ✕, Escape or reclicking the token), custom wallpaper upload (centre-cropped and scaled to the screen's device pixels before storing, `theme-wallpaper-fit.js`; animated GIFs up to 40 MB (4 MB in Chrome Web Store builds, which strip `unlimitedStorage`) are stored as uploaded so they keep animating, never flattened to JPEG; large JSON-imported wallpapers too, and wallpapers stored before fitting existed, active or in custom themes, are fitted once in the background and stored back in place, `theme-wallpaper-migrate.js`; SVG presets never) and caching, preset wallpapers (the six from Settings; a theme stores and exports the picked one by id, `wallpaperPreset`; a theme's own wallpaper shows only while that theme is active and never replaces the Settings wallpaper, which every theme without its own shows), import/export as `.ctme` theme files (also imported straight from the theme menu's Import Theme..., below Customize Theme...): a ZIP named after the theme (`Test Theme` -> `test_theme.ctme`) holding `theme.json` and the wallpaper image (`theme-ctme.js`); older `.json` theme files still import. Hovering over any element in the customizer preview displays a floating tooltip (`#cx-preview-tooltip`) showing the element name, the token colouring the text under the pointer (glyph hit test) and the fill tokens. Includes dual Liquid Glass sliders: **Blur Strength** (0–40px, default 12px, `--cx-glass-blur`) and **Transparency** (10%–100%, default 65%, `--cx-glass-opacity`). With Liquid Glass on, a custom theme is a recoloured Liquid Glass theme (`theme-glass-tokens.js`): surface, secondary, elevated, nav and input fills are its own colours at the Transparency opacity (scaled like the preset's 0.72 surface), and hover, border, button, table and badge layers are its text colour at the preset's overlay strengths. Custom themes in the menu have Edit (✎) and Delete (✕); editing saves by the theme's id, so a rename never duplicates it. Saving a new theme checks existing themes by name (case-insensitive), safely stores wallpapers across `localStorage` and `chrome.storage.local` quota limits, and immediately applies the theme via `ConnectifyTheme.applyTheme()`.
- **Wallpaper Layer** (`theme-wallpaper-layer.css`, `theme-wallpaper-dim.js`): the wallpaper is one fixed layer under the page (`html::before`, 6px past the viewport), blurred 2px; `<html>`/`<body>` paint only the canvas. With light text (dark themes not in light tone) it is dimmed by the least black overlay that keeps white text >= 4.5:1 on its brightest sampled area (`--cx-wallpaper-dim`); with dark text (Light, light-tone and light custom themes) it is brightened by the least white overlay that keeps the theme's `--cx-text-primary` >= 4.5:1 on its darkest sampled area (`--cx-wallpaper-brighten`, re-measured when the theme's ink changes). Both are measured once per wallpaper and cached in `connectify:wallpaper_dim`. Labels and other text get no text shadow (a 1px label shadow made text hard to read).
- **Custom Wallpaper Pass-Through (every dark theme)**: layout containers (`#main-content`, `.cvr-c-app-layout*`, `.v-app`, `.v-ui`, `.v-scrollable`) that hold tiles (`:has(.eds-c-tile)`; detail views such as notices keep an opaque surface) and wrappers that only hold tiles (`.v-panel`, `.v-panel-content`, `.cvr-u-padding--md` with `:has(.eds-c-tile)`) are transparent, so the canvas or wallpaper shows in Connect's native 15px padding around tiles exactly as in Default. Tiles stay opaque outside Liquid Glass.
- **Reading Views**: notice and discussion views (`cnt-notice-view`, `cnt-discussion-item-view`, dialogs) set `data-connectea-reading-view`. Solid themes: the canvas is opaque and outranks the wallpaper on `<html>`/`<body>`. Liquid Glass (`theme-glass-reading.css`): no reading panel; the page, the frame holding the view (`.cnt-ng-zone > div`) and the view are clear, so the text sits on the wallpaper, dimmed or brightened for the theme's text (Wallpaper Layer); the wallpaper layer stays visible. Notice and dialog text uses the primary ink (`--cx-text-primary`, cream `#fbf1c7` in Quantum Dark), never the secondary; feed cards keep the secondary ink.
- **Light Mode over a Wallpaper**: feed tabs, sub-headers and toolbars have no panel (the white 90% contrast panels were removed); their labels keep crisp dark ink (`#334155` / `#0f172a`) on the brightened wallpaper.
- **Parity Defect & Assessment Outlines**: `.cvr-u-padding--md` keeps Connect's native 15px padding (clear, never filled or zeroed, never `display: none`) in every theme; Connectify's own additions (the glass statistics strip) draw outside their box on `::before` so Connect rows keep their native size. Parity test checks button and input styling across all themes, ignoring only panels, cards, tiles, and modals under Liquid Glass.
- **Scenario Values in Dark Mode**:
  - Low: `#e1eaf3` on dark card.
  - Middle / Expected: High-contrast white `#ffffff` on `#334c65`.
  - High: High-contrast vibrant green `#4ade80` on dark card.
- **Containers & Padding**: a container and its own padding are one surface and keep very similar colours; the audit skips adjacent-contrast between an element that fills its parent's content box and that parent, and warns (`PADDING_COLOR_MISMATCH`) when their colours differ. Icon-only buttons (class containing `icon`, or `.mat-icon-button`, even when bordered) keep native padding and size so webfont glyphs are never offset (`theme-icon-buttons.css`). Dialog title, content, footer and `cnt-*-dialog` host (`.connect-dialog`) regions are transparent over the dialog's one surface (`theme-dialog-surface.css`), and the reading-view canvas rule skips dialog contents; a `cnt-paginator` that wraps the table keeps native layout (only the paging bar is flexed).
- **User Menu** (`theme-user-menu.css`, every dark theme): the avatar button has no fill of its own (the bar shows through, hover highlights); the user dropdown is one surface, so its header (avatar, name, role) and Sign Out row are clear with `--cx-text-primary` ink (>= 5:1, Test 194). Sign Out keeps Connect's geometry (full-width 50px bar, no border or radius, bold caption): the generic button rules skip `cvr-c-primary-navigation__button--*` and `.cvr-c-primary-navigation__user-controls` buttons (colours only).
- **Card Header Buttons & Dividers**: card header links (Classes `View All`, `.mat-card-button` in `mat-card-header`) use `--cx-button-bg` / `--cx-button-text` / `--cx-button-hover`, and the class-coloured `.mat-divider--primary` under card headers uses `--cx-border-strong`, in every dark theme (colours only).
- **Service Bar `+`**: the reveal-menu circle (`.c-control-panel-button .mat-icon-button--size-sm`) uses the primary button colours instead of Connect's class colour (colours only).
- **Semantic Classes**: Use semantic classes (`.cx-settings-label`, `.cx-pred-scenario-value--mid`, `.cx-settings-col-header`) instead of hardcoded inline dark styles.
- **Dynamic Surface Adaptation**: Use `data-connectea-surface` and `data-connectea-ink` on unstyled surfaces. Avoid inline style mutations.
- **Light Tone**: a dark-classed theme with dark ink (grey Liquid Glass, light custom themes) sets `data-connectea-tone="light"` on `<html>`; `theme-light-tone.css` re-inks data colours (category labels, scenario labels, High pills/cards, chart keys, date warning, Attendance labels) at OKLCH lightness 0.34 keeping hue and chroma, turns Connect's dark status pills (Overdue, locked, blue-light) into light tints with dark ink, and edges the sidebar handle in the ink colour. The Weakness Analyzer plot (dark `#333333` in every other theme, set in CSS, never inline) turns clear with an ink hairline: its labels, ring percentages and messages take the ink, and its scores and warning, like the Settings delete ✕ and "Cache cleared!" note, go to OKLCH 0.30 because they sit straight on the drawer glass (Test 186). Links use `--cx-accent-link` (each preset's accent-hover; derived themes ensure 5:1), never the raw accent; avatar initials use `--cx-avatar-text`.
- **Typography Bolding Parity**: Dark and light modes enforce 1:1 bolding parity (`font-weight: 700 !important` on task titles `.cvr-c-task__details .v-label:first-child`, `.cvr-c-task__title`, group headers, task marks, and cohort stats `strong` tags; `font-weight: 400 !important` on secondary labels, out-of text, and subject summary marks).
- **Login Screen Dark Mode Suppression & Post-Login Restoration**: Dark mode is strictly toggled off on `https://connect.det.wa.edu.au/login` (removing `.connectea-dark` and temporarily setting storage to `light`, recording `connectea:theme:restore_dark`) to prevent broken styles on the account selector. Upon successful login and navigation to normal Connect pages, dark mode is automatically restored.

## 3. Sidebar & Workspace Geometry
- **Toggle Handle (`#connectify-sidebar-handle`)**: Sleek 30px width handle with directional chevron (`❮` / `❯`). When the drawer is closed, anchors directly to Connect's native left navigation sidebar (`height >= 120`, excluding primary top menus) scrolling in sync with Connect's sidebar via `position: absolute`, or `position: fixed` if the sidebar is fixed/sticky. Switches to `position: fixed` docked to the drawer edge when the drawer is open. Falls back to dynamic viewport vertical centering below top navigation bars (`rect.top <= 25`) if no Connect sidebar is present.
- **Drawer Width (`#connectify-sidebar`)**: Compact 310px; expands to **900px** (`.cx-tool-active`) when any tool is active.
- **Drawer Slide Synchronization**: `#connectify-sidebar` and `#connectify-sidebar-handle` animate in lockstep with matching `0.2s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` transitions using `transform: translateX(-100%)` to `0`, preventing clipping.
- **Left-Edge Push & Hover Dismissal**: Moving mouse to `clientX <= 12` expands the drawer. Moving mouse outside collapses it after 120ms debounce when no tool is active. When a tool is active, hover collapse is suppressed and clicking outside collapses it. Returning from an active tool panel retains the launcher menu (`justReturnedToMenu`) without hover collapse. Clicking the handle always wins over hover: after a click-close nothing reopens the drawer until the pointer leaves the handle and the edge zone, and a click within 400ms of a hover-open keeps it open.
- **Canonical Launcher Button Sequence**:
  1. `connectify-target-toggle` (Target ATAR)
  2. `connectify-grade-toggle` (Target Grade)
  3. `connectify-predictor-toggle` (Predictor)
  4. `connectify-progress-toggle` (Year in Progress)
  5. `connectify-estimate-toggle` (ATAR Estimate)
  6. `connectify-weakness-toggle` (Weakness Analyzer)
  7. `connectify-categories-toggle` (Settings)
- **Secondary Tool Buttons**: Settings and Weakness Analyzer feature clean white buttons (`#ffffff`) in light mode with centered text (`text-align: center !important; justify-content: center !important;`).
- **Workspace Navigation**: Dedicated `← Back to Menu` navigation header (`.cx-back-menu`) positioned at the top right of the header; single active tool policy enforced via `connectify-open` event; outside click and Escape key dismissal.

## 4. Outcome Meter & Tooltip Architecture
- **Bar Dimensions**: Width **18px**, total height **56px** (2–3x scale).
- **Segments**:
  - Baseline track: 4 segments (10px height each).
  - Tier mapping: Red ($< \text{Low}$), Orange ($\text{Low} \le S < \text{Mid}$), Yellow ($\text{Mid} \le S < \text{High}$), Green ($S \ge \text{High}$).
  - Active segment opacity: 100% in dark mode against inactive track `#334155`.
- **Secret Purple Breakout**: When score $> 1.10 \times \text{High}$, bar erupts with a double-height 5th Purple segment (`.connectea-active-purple`, height 20px). **The purple breakout threshold is strictly secret**—never disclosed in legends or tooltips.
- **Critical Shortfall**: When score $\le \text{Low} - 10\%$ (additive), bar renders 0 segments (empty) with pulsing red alarm glow (`.connectea-outcome-critical`).
- **Suppression Rules**:
  - Suppress outcome bars on incomplete or unmarked assessments (`Number.isFinite(mark)` check).
  - Suppress on a subject's first *marked* assessment unless a numeric subject baseline exists: prior data means an earlier marked row in the card or, for a Semester 2 task, a marked Semester 1 result. A task's semester is its own week, not its card's: **Semester 1 is every task before Term 2 Week 7** (chronological week 19, school week 17; `ConnectifyParseHelpers.taskSemester`), so the Semester 1 tasks a Semester 2 panel lists stay Semester 1 tasks and the first of them gets no meter (unmarked rows never count). First tasks of a type render normally when prior subject data exists.
  - When baselines are cleared/removed, `saveBaselines()` thoroughly purges all legacy storage candidate keys to prevent zombie baseline resurrection, strictly suppressing outcome bars on first tasks.
- **Interaction**: Segments must have `pointer-events: none !important;` to eliminate hover boundary thrashing. Handled via singleton floating tooltip (`#connectea-outcome-tooltip`) using viewport-fixed coordinates. Outcome bars and segments must **never** set the native HTML `title` attribute to prevent overlapping dual tooltips.

## 5. Cold-Start & Mathematical Modeling
- **Logarithmic Headroom Above 80%**: Taper achievable gains proportionally to remaining headroom $\frac{100 - \text{Score}}{20}$.
- **Calibrated Variance Scaling on Predictions**: Low delta scales moderately with variance ($\Delta_{\text{Low}} = \min(18, \max(1.5, \frac{\sigma^2}{20} + 0.45\sigma + \Delta_{\text{penalty}}))$) and high delta as $\Delta_{\text{High}} = \min(8.5, \max(1.5, 0.88\sigma))$, preventing excessive spread on volatile subjects while preserving realistic downside/upside bounds.
- **New School Year Baselines** (`year-rollover.js`): when every class on the outlines page has a new name and the new set is a later year (by year level or calendar year, or never seen before when titles carry neither), last year's averages of assessment types and subjects (the cache's final state over the last snapshot; grade cache marks preferred, only last year's classes) become the baselines for types that are still categories and subjects still present (matched year-independently, e.g. `11 Chemistry ATAR` -> `12 Chemistry ATAR`). The class registry (`class-semesters.js`) triggers it as soon as it sees a wholly new class set. Connect never shows last year's marks again, so the cache is the only copy: last year's cached results are archived (`connectify:year_archive:`, last two years) before being cleared. Previous baselines are backed up, and viewing an earlier year never triggers it or enters the registry.
- **Show This Semester's Classes Only** (`semester-filter.js`, Settings checkbox, off by default): the semester is always the detected one (no picker): Semester 2 only once a class shows a final A–E grade; marks never count (Semester 2 panels list the Semester 1 tasks and a cumulative percent from day one, and a Semester 2 task can be marked before Semester 1 grades are final). The Target ATAR / Grade planners use the same start rule. The other semester's outline tiles are hidden, only this semester's cards refresh the cache (the other semester's cached results stay frozen and are still used), and every sidebar tool reads the filtered cache (`collectClasses`, `readCourses`), never the tiles.
- **Settings Cold-Start Guidance**: When a subject lacks scores or baselines, display guidance card with a direct "Go to Settings" shortcut. Dynamically inject baseline inputs when custom categories are created.

## 6. Runtime & DOM Safety
- **World Isolation**: Respect `MAIN` world data bridge (`data.js` stamps `dataset.connectifyStats`) vs `ISOLATED` world UI scripts.
- **Zero-Flicker Observers**: Page-wide observation and periodic work go through `ConnectifyScheduler` (`onMutations` with a filter and `mute`, `every` gated on activity, visibility and open panels), never a new body observer or `setInterval`; filter out `style` attribute mutations; track user activity with `ConnectifyIsUserActive()`.
- **Strict Deduplication**: Purge redundant DOM elements on SPA transitions. Ensure all tool launchers and panels export standard `panelRefs` (`toggleBtn`, `panel`).

## 7. Panel Lifecycle & Backwards Compatibility
- **No Unauthorized Auto-Expansion**: Panels and subject outlines are **not allowed to be automatically expanded without user involvement** (e.g. clicking a button such as "Expand All", "Expand Subject Outlines", or an individual subject accordion). The **sole exception** is initial expansion on page load if auto expand all is checked (`connectify:auto_expand`).
- **Settings Cache Backwards Compatibility**: Backwards compatibility must be maintained with cached data from previous versions. Stored user preferences, baselines, category configurations, and scaling calibrations must gracefully resolve legacy keys (`connectea:`, `cx-`, `connectify:`) without data loss.

## 8. Subsystem Cache Invalidation & Algorithm Versioning Standards
- **Independent Subsystem Invalidation Keys**: Subsystems (Predictor, Results, Settings, Cohort) must maintain separate, independent invalidation version strings (e.g. `PREDICTOR_ALGO_VERSION`, `RESULTS_ALGO_VERSION`, `SETTINGS_ALGO_VERSION`, `COHORT_ALGO_VERSION`) and corresponding storage keys (`connectify:cache_version:<subsystem>`).
- **Mandatory Version Increment on Algorithm Changes**: Whenever mathematical algorithms, scraping heuristics, or data models are modified within any subsystem, that subsystem's specific invalidation version value **must** be updated.
- **Isolated Subsystem Cache Clearing**: Clearing a subsystem cache must be strictly scoped to that subsystem's domain. Updating or invalidating the predictor cache must never purge user settings or cohort data, and vice versa.
- **Memoized Row Rendering**: Cohort panels and outcome bars must memoize render state (`_lastMark`, `_lastStatsKey`, `_lastEstimatedSize`, `taskType`, `baselinesSig`). Skip PCHIP splines, historical aggregation, and DOM rebuilds if data has not changed, preventing frame drops during user scrolling.
- **Prediction Cache Synchronization & Expansion Pipeline**: Changing a task's category, saving category settings keywords, or saving baselines immediately refreshes the prediction cache (`updatePredictionCache`): stale predictions are dropped at once and every request in the same task shares one recompute and one cohort pass, run after the change handler so the dropdown never waits (Test 196). When a subject grade update is detected, the subject is marked stale, and upon subsequent expansion, its cached predictions are refreshed via the expansion pipeline without background DOM scanning.
- **Settings Save Button Styling Parity**: Assessment Categories Save Changes (`#cx-cat-save`) and Baselines Save (`saveBtn`) strictly share the identical canonical primary blue styling (`#2563eb`, white text, 6px 14px padding).

## 9. Documentation Synchronization & Brevity Standards
- **Continuous Documentation Sync**: Always update all files in `docs/` with every change made to their relevant subsystems, modules, or features.
- **Extreme Brevity & High Signal**: Documentation must be kept extremely brief, concise, and focused strictly on useful architectural, mathematical, and implementation facts. Avoid fluff or redundant prose.
- **Strict Line Count Constraint**: Every JS and CSS file in `src/` must be **$\le 250$ lines**. `test_suite.js` and the `scripts/` test tooling are exempt. Markdown files in `docs/` must remain strictly **$\le 150$ lines each** at all times; Markdown outside `docs/` (README, changelogs, `overview.md`, `design_rules.md`) has no line limit.
- **New Features, New Files**: New features go in new, self-contained files (registered in `manifest.json` in dependency order). Never append a new feature to an existing module.
- **Theme Scoping**: Every theme selector must be scoped to a theme hook (`.connectea-dark`, `data-connectea-*`, or a `cx`/`connectify` id/class). Unscoped rules that change native Connect styling are not allowed, apart from the explicit allowlist in `test_suite.js`.
- **Token-Routed Colours**: dark and glass rules (theme, sidebar, ATAR, Predictor, Progress and injected cohort styles) write theme colours as `var(--cx-<token>, <Dark literal>)` (or `color-mix` of tokens) so custom themes reach every surface; only semantic colours (status, outcome segments, categories, success/warning cards) stay literal (Test 167). A wallpaper also paints the root, so short pages show it below the content.

## 10. Test Integrity & Direct Implementation
- **User Instruction on Test Maintenance**: "In the future, when I ask you to change something, please change any tests that explicitly assert against that thing instead of using weird workarounds which may cause many more problems later, as that test was likely created out of a misunderstanding. Please push and commit always."
- **Direct Test Updates**: When requested to change functionality, typography, markup, styling, or behavior, directly update the implementation and any tests that explicitly assert against that specific item.
- **No Workarounds to Appease Tests**: Never use indirect workarounds (such as initializing an element with an old value only to overwrite it with `.textContent`, or adding redundant attributes) just to appease an outdated test assertion. Outdated test assertions were likely written under an earlier requirement or misunderstanding and must be updated directly to assert the newly specified behavior.
