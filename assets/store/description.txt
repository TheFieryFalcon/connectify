Connectify turns Western Australia's Connect portal into a study dashboard for Year 11 and 12 students. See where you stand in your cohort, predict your ATAR, and find out exactly what you need on your next assessments. Works for every WA high school, right inside the pages you already use.

📈 KNOW WHERE YOU STAND
• Cohort rank: estimates your percentile and rank on every assessment (e.g. Top 4.2%) from the published boxplots.
• Outcome meter: a colour-coded bar beside each mark shows how the result compares with what was expected of you.
• Grade alerts: get notified when a class's results change, with one click to jump straight to it.

🔮 PLAN AHEAD
• ATAR predictor: applies published TISC scaling data to your school marks, with Low, Mid and High scenarios.
• Grade predictions: forecasts your upcoming results from how you've done in that subject and on that type of task.
• Target planner: enter your dream ATAR or subject grade and see the score you need on every remaining assessment.

📊 SPOT YOUR WEAKNESSES
• Weakness Analyzer: a radar chart of your results by assessment type, so you know what to study first.
• Progress bars on every subject show how much of the year's weighting is already done.
• Year in Progress graphs track your trajectory across the year.

🎨 MAKE IT YOURS
• Seven themes, including AMOLED Black and frosted Liquid Glass, or build your own from just 6 colours.
• Pick a preset wallpaper or upload your own.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs locally. Your marks never leave your browser: no accounts, no tracking, no servers.
